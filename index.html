🩺 PLANT DOCTOR

Something Wrong With Your Plant?

Don't worry. Identify common plant problems and learn what your plant may need.

🍂

Yellow Leaves

Too much water, poor drainage or insufficient light can make leaves yellow.

💡 Check soil before watering
🥀

Drooping Plant

Drooping leaves can happen because of watering stress, heat or root problems.

💡 Check moisture & sunlight
🐛

Tiny Pests

Small insects, sticky leaves or tiny spots may indicate pests.

💡 Check under the leaves
☀️

Brown Leaves

Harsh sunlight, dry air or irregular watering can cause brown edges.

💡 Keep watering consistent
🩺
Plant Doctor Tip

Before treating any problem, check three things: light, soil moisture and drainage.

🌱 EASY PLANT CARE

Simple Care for Healthier Plants

A little care every day can help your plants stay fresh, strong and beautiful.

01
☀️

Give Them Light

Give every plant the right amount of sunlight. Not every plant needs direct sun.

02
💧

Water Wisely

Touch the soil before watering. Overwatering can harm roots.

03
🌱

Healthy Soil

Use loose, nutrient-rich and well-draining soil for healthier roots.

04
✂️

Trim & Clean

Remove dry leaves and flowers to encourage fresh and healthy growth.

05
🔍

Check Regularly

Take one minute to inspect leaves, soil and stems for early problems.

06
💚

Be Consistent

Plants don't need perfection. Small regular care helps them thrive.
