Common Issues & Solutions

💧

Overwatering

Mushy yellow leaves and soft stem base.

Fix: Stop watering, check pot drainage holes, and allow soil to dry completely.
🌵

Underwatering

Dry crispy tips and soil shrinking from pot edges.

Fix: Give a thorough deep soak until water drains out from the bottom holes.
🐛

Pest Infestation

White cotton-like spots or sticky sap on leaves.

Fix: Spray dilute Neem oil spray or rub alcohol on affected areas with cotton.

🌿 DIY Natural Organic Neem Spray

A simple homemade pesticide safe for indoor pets and plants.

Ingredients:

• 1 Liter Water

• 1 Teaspoon Pure Organic Neem Oil

• 1/2 Teaspoon Mild Dish Soap

How to Apply:

Mix well in a spray bottle. Spray leaves once every week in the early morning or evening.
