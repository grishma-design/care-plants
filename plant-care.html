4 Pillars of Plant Health

☀️

Sunlight

Rotate plants every fortnight so all sides receive balanced light exposure evenly.

🌊

Watering

Always check top 2 inches of soil with finger test before watering to prevent root rot.

🪴

Soil Nutrition

Use well-draining potting mix blended with compost or vermicompost every month.

💨

Air Flow

Keep plant leaves free of dust using a soft damp cloth to maximize photosynthesis.
